As **technical lead** in the **ORIGAMI** project (European Defence Fund), at **IMEC & VUB**, I work on **drone localization and navigation**: reliable autonomy in the real world requires robust **multi-sensor fusion**, strong **localization**, and perception that holds up under harsh conditions.

Focus
======
* **Full drone platform**: architected and integrated hardware, sensing, and autonomy software
* **Multi-sensor fusion** for autonomous drones (RGB, LiDAR, multispectral, IMU, GNSS)
* **Localization** and state estimation in 3D environments as part of a deployable autonomy stack
* **World models** for perception and autonomous navigation

What I’m working on
======
* Leading the technical development of the drone autonomy stack.
* Developing world-model-based methods for autonomous navigation.
* Fusing complementary signals across sensors to improve robustness to missing/noisy inputs.
* Building deployment-minded systems: latency-aware, stable, and testable on real platforms.
